Frequently asked questions

How much does color grading cost?

It depends on the project. We usually charge per project, but sometimes it's per day or per minute, depending on the client. Tell us about yours and we'll send you a quote.

How long does color grading take?

It depends a lot on the project. On average:

  • 30-second commercial3 days
  • Music video5 to 6 days
  • Short film17 days
  • Feature film1 month and 2 days

These times already include about 2 days to receive the project from the editor, conform it and deliver.

Do you have availability for my project?

Usually, yes. There are five colorists on the team, so a new project doesn't have to wait for another one to finish. Send us your delivery date and we'll confirm.
