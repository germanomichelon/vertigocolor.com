About
We are color.
The best Color Grading company in Brazil.
As a Brazilian company, we are praised by our work with low and high budget projects, from fiction, documentary, advertisement and even institucional.
Vertigo Color was born from a singular obsession with the cinematic image. When I first set out to elevate my own video productions, the endless complexities of lighting, sound design, and camera gear felt overwhelming—but color grading immediately clicked. Encouraged by industry mentors like Filippo Cinotti who proved that a dedicated career in color was possible, I went all-in and invested everything I had into mastering the craft. After two years of relentless grinding and honing those skills, I realized it was time to scale.
The turning point came when I joined forces with a business partner who brought over a decade of post-production experience to the table. He had always envisioned building a specialized, high-level color grading studio, and he brought the name with him: Vertigo Color. By combining fresh, obsessive dedication with deeply rooted technical expertise, we built a post-production house entirely around the art and science of the grade.
Today, Vertigo Color stands as one of the most premium and sought-after color grading studios in Brazil—but our focus is global. Operating out of South America allows us to offer North American and European markets a massive advantage: world-class, high-end finishing at a highly competitive rate. We deliver the uncompromising quality of a top-tier studio, making elite color grading accessible and economically smart for international productions.
